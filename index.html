Alerts and Keep Screen Awake work while this app is open on screen. If the phone locks or the app is backgrounded, Android may still pause the timer depending on the device — keep the app open in the foreground for the most reliable alerts.

Line Codes (QR + NFC)

Each line gets one code, usable as a printed QR label and/or a written NFC tag. Scanning inside the app fills the line and starts the timer — a normal camera or NFC reader just sees a random code.
